MEMORANDUM — DIVISION OF ENFORCEMENT

Re: Harborview Capital Partners — trading records review
File No. HO-14427
Date: 09/18/2023

Staff reviewed account statements produced by JPMorgan Chase for brokerage account 4471-882910 and a second account, 5520-117384, both opened by Jonathan Mercer in 2019.

Between March 15, 2022 and March 21, 2022, Daniel K. Okafor bought 41,000 shares of BLCT through account 5520-117384. Mr. Okafor's email, dokafor.private@protonmail.com, appears on the account opening documents together with phone (203) 555-0148 and SSN 471-38-2290.

Ms. Priya Raman, a compliance analyst at Harborview, told staff she raised concerns with Mercer in April 2022. Raman stated that "J. Mercer" approved the transfers personally. Her supervisor, the firm's chief compliance officer, did not escalate the issue.

The BLCT orders were routed through Meridian Clearing, and a former colleague, Whitcombe, confirmed the timing to staff.

Nasdaq surveillance flagged the BLCT trades on March 23, 2022. The matter was referred to FINRA and the SEC.
